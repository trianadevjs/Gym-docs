# MVP Scope

The MVP is centered on the **Reception** role. Reception is the operational core and
produces the data (members, memberships, payments) that every later report depends on.

## In the MVP

- Staff authentication (login) with roles
- Gym (location) and plan catalog setup
- Register a member at the front desk
- Search members by first and last name
- See a member's next payment date
- Record a payment for a plan (day, week, month)
- Cash cut for a reception shift / period

## Later phases

- **Manager reports:** active memberships, comparison with past periods, current
  occupancy, cash cut and revenue per period
- **Owner reports:** the same metrics aggregated across all gyms
- **Member features:** self sign-up and login, online payments, QR code for gym
  access, attendance calendar and workout streaks
